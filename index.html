Gull Bomber Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Gull Bomber game uses, why, and the choices you have. It covers the game on Android (Google Play) and iPhone (App Store). Gull Bomber is published by Landsweeper Games ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at jamsweeper93@googlemail.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account to play.
  • Your coins, skins, scores and settings are saved on your phone. On Android, if you sign in to Google Play Games, a backup copy is also kept in your Google account.
  • If you sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone), your scores and achievements are sent to that service's leaderboards.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to show and measure ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you're asked for consent first, and on iPhone you're asked whether to allow tracking. You can change your choices at any time.

Information saved on your device

The game stores your progress on your phone so it's there next time you play. This includes your coin balance, unlocked and equipped skins, best scores, Daily Challenge attempts, missions, sound setting and similar game settings. We don't collect it and it isn't sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the copy on your phone.

Google Play Games Services (Android)

On Android, Gull Bomber uses Google Play Games Services for leaderboards, achievements and saved games. If you're signed in to Play Games:

  • Google receives your scores and achievements and links them to your Play Games profile, so your gamer name and score can appear on the public leaderboards.
  • The game asks Google for your leaderboard rank to decide whether you've earned the Daily Challenge reward.
  • A backup of your game progress (the same information saved on your phone) is stored in your Google account as a Play Games saved game, so it can be restored if you reinstall or change phones.

Google handles this information under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile, visibility and saved games in the Google Play Games settings on your device.

Game Center (iPhone)

On iPhone, if you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards with your Game Center name. Apple handles this under its own policy: Apple Privacy Policy. You can manage Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Advertising

Gull Bomber shows ads using Google AdMob. This includes optional ads you can choose to watch for rewards, and may include occasional full-screen ads in Free Flight. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version
  • approximate location based on your IP address
  • how you interact with ads

If you're in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy settings on the main menu or at the bottom of the Shop screen. US players can use the same link to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information. If you don't consent, you may still see ads that aren't personalised.

On iPhone, the game also asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising ID isn't shared and ads are less personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Sharing the game

The "Invite friends" and "Challenge a friend" buttons open your phone's own share menu with a short message and a link to the game. You choose who to send it to. We don't see who you share with or what you send.

Purchases

You can buy a one-off "Remove ads" upgrade. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms. We don't receive your payment details. The game only checks with the store whether the upgrade has been bought, so it can switch ads off and restore the upgrade if you reinstall.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't run our own servers or analytics, and we don't receive your personal information from Google or Apple.
  • The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

How long information is kept and how to delete it

  • On your phone: kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Play Games saved game (Android): kept in your Google account until you delete it. You can remove it in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.
  • Leaderboards and achievements: kept by Google or Apple under their own policies. You can hide or reset your profile in Play Games or Game Center settings.
  • Advertising data: collected and kept by Google under its own policy. You can withdraw consent at any time from Ad privacy settings in the game, turn off tracking on iPhone, or reset your advertising ID.

The companies we work with (Google for ads, Play Games and Google Play, and Apple for Game Center and the App Store) only receive information as described above, and handle it under their own privacy policies, which give at least the protection described here.

Children

Gull Bomber is not designed for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to how it's used. Because we don't hold your personal information ourselves, most of these requests are best made to Google or Apple through your account settings or their privacy tools. We're happy to help if you contact us. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, which in the UK is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
