Gull Bomber Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Gull Bomber game uses, why, and the choices you have. Gull Bomber is published by Landsweeper Games ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at jamsweeper93@googlemail.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account to play.
  • Your coins, skins, scores and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • If you sign in to Google Play Games, your scores are sent to Google's leaderboards.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to show and measure ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

Information saved on your device

The game stores your progress on your phone so it's there next time you play. This includes your coin balance, unlocked and equipped skins, best scores, Daily Challenge attempts, sound setting and similar game settings. This information stays on your device. We don't collect it and it isn't sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Google Play Games Services

Gull Bomber uses Google Play Games Services for its leaderboards. If you're signed in to Play Games, Google receives your scores and links them to your Play Games profile, so your gamer name and score can appear on the public leaderboards. The game also asks Google for your leaderboard rank to decide whether you've earned the Daily Challenge reward. Google handles this information under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games settings on your device.

Advertising

Gull Bomber shows ads using Google AdMob. This includes optional ads you can choose to watch for rewards, and occasional full-screen ads in Free Flight. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version
  • approximate location based on your IP address
  • how you interact with ads

If you're in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy settings on the main menu or at the bottom of the Hangar screen. US players can use the same link to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information. If you don't consent, you may still see ads that aren't personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

You can buy a one-off "Remove ads" upgrade. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms. We don't receive your payment details. The game only checks with Google Play whether the upgrade has been bought, so it can switch ads off and restore the upgrade if you reinstall.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't run our own servers or analytics, and we don't receive your personal information from Google.
  • The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Children

Gull Bomber is not designed for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to how it's used. Because we don't hold your personal information ourselves, most of these requests are best made to Google through your Google Account settings or Google's privacy tools. We're happy to help if you contact us. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, which in the UK is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
